WHO I AM

A solo Perth founder, building automation for local real estate agencies

SmartFlow Automation is a one-person operation. I started it after noticing something as a property buyer in Perth: enquiries submitted through realestate.com.au, domain.com.au and agency websites often went unanswered for hours. Sometimes longer.

As a customer, that experience is easy to walk away from. You move on to the next listing or the next agent before anyone responds. I started to realise how many genuine, high-intent leads agencies were quietly losing every week, simply because their teams were in appointments, buried in admin, or had already finished for the day.

That observation led me to build and test automation systems designed specifically for real estate enquiry handling: faster first-response, automated follow-up, appointment scheduling and admin reduction. The goal is practical: give agents more time for the work that genuinely requires a person. Conversations, relationships and closing deals.

When you work with SmartFlow, you work directly with me. Not a sales rep, not an account manager, not a junior developer. I scope your project, I build the system, and I'm the person you call when you have a question.

I don't sell software. I build custom systems around how your agency already works, and I stay involved to make sure they keep working.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does the setup take?

Most setups are completed within 7–14 business days. This includes the discovery call, workflow build, testing, and go-live. More complex custom builds on the Scale plan may take up to 3 weeks. You'll have a clear timeline from day one.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

None at all. We handle every part of the technical setup on your behalf. You'll just need to provide access to the tools you currently use (email, CRM, calendar) and we take it from there. If you can send an email, you can use our system.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. All plans are month-to-month after the initial setup. You can cancel with 30 days' notice at any time. We keep clients through results, not contracts.

What tools and platforms do you integrate with?

We integrate with a wide range of tools including Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, REA Group, Domain.com.au, and most major CRMs used in Australian real estate: Rex, Agentbox, PropertyMe, and others. Book a call and we'll confirm compatibility with your existing stack before you commit.

What's included in the setup fee?

The $995 setup fee covers your discovery and scoping session, custom workflow build, integration configuration, full testing and QA, and go-live support. Everything is done for you. You don't lift a finger during setup.

How quickly will I see results?

Most agencies notice a difference within the first week, particularly around lead response speed and time saved on admin. Within 30 days you should see measurable improvements in lead engagement rates and hours saved per agent per week.

Can you work with our existing CRM?

Yes, in most cases. We build on top of the tools you already use rather than replacing them. If your CRM has an API or integration capability (which most modern ones do), we can connect it. We'll confirm this during the discovery call before you commit to anything.

What if I want to add or change automations later?

Growth and Scale plan clients can request workflow changes and additions at any time. Minor adjustments are included in your monthly plan. Larger custom builds are scoped and quoted separately. Starter clients can upgrade at any time.
